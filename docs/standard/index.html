The price of AI inference, measured by buying it. Each weekday between 14:00 and 15:00 UTC, from London, Hunterson buys the same test job from every provider serving DeepSeek V4.1-Flash through one marketplace, and keeps the receipts. The figures are the averages of what the middle half of qualifying providers charged, per million input and per million output tokens. Standard covers models that use 30 billion active parameters or fewer for each token. A token is about three quarters of a word.
