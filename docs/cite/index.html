How to cite Hunterson

Every figure has a permanent address and a line ready to paste. Citing it is free and needs no permission; using it in a contract, a financial product or an index needs a free registration (the licence).

The line to paste

Hunterson Standard Inference, Europe, 13 November 2026: input $0.1949, output $0.8111 per million tokens. https://hunterson.org/standard/2026-11-13/

This is the line for the latest assessment, 13 November 2026. Each day's page has its own, under the figure. On a day with no figure, the day's page says why; cite the last day that has one.

In a paper or report

Hunterson (2026). Hunterson Standard Inference, Europe, assessment of 13 November 2026. Published by Malcolm Hunter, trading as Hunterson. https://hunterson.org/standard/2026-11-13/. Methodology version 1.0.

As BibTeX:

@misc{hunterson-standard-2026-11-13,
  author = {{Hunterson}},
  title = {Hunterson Standard Inference, Europe: assessment of 13 November 2026},
  year = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://hunterson.org/standard/2026-11-13/}},
  note = {Input \$0.1949, output \$0.8111 per million tokens. Methodology version 1.0}
}

The addresses

  • A day's assessment, the address to cite: https://hunterson.org/standard/YYYY-MM-DD/. It is written once and never changed.
  • The latest assessment: hunterson.org/standard/, rewritten each weekday.
  • A day's chart card, 1200 by 630 pixels, with its date on it: https://hunterson.org/og/standard/YYYY-MM-DD.png. The latest is 13 November 2026.
  • Every figure, to download: CSV and JSON; the latest only, for programs: latest.json; and a feed. What each column means is on the data page.

The licence, in one line

Free to use with credit: “Source: Hunterson Standard Inference, Europe, hunterson.org”. Use in contracts, financial products or indices needs a free registration: hunterson.org/licence.

The monthly report

Hunterson Monthly comes out on the first Tuesday of each month, from 1 December 2026: the month's delivered price, the receipt gap, the delivery rate, the reference and its challenger, one chart, and every correction and rule change. Notices of a change to the rules are sent with it, or sooner when one is announced. To receive it, email malcolm@hunterson.org with the subject “Subscribe”. Your address is kept in the project's mailbox, used only for these emails and never shared. Reply “Unsubscribe” to any of them and it is deleted.

Questions and corrections

Write to malcolm@hunterson.org. A published figure is never changed; a correction is published as a new, dated entry (the methodology).
